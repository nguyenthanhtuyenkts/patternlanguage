### Problem
>People will not feel comfortable in their houses unless a group of houses forms a cluster, with the public land between them jointly owned by all the householders.

### Solution
>Arrange houses to form very rough, but identifiable clusters of 8 to 12 households around some common land and paths. Arrange the clusters so that anyone can walk through them, without feeling like a trespasser.

### Related Patterns
... the fundamental unit of organization within the neighborhood - [[14. Identifiable Neighborhood]] - is the cluster of a dozen houses. By varying the density and composition of different clusters, this pattern may also help to generate the [[29. Density Rings]], [[35. Household Mix]], and [[36. Degrees of Publicness]].

Use this pattern as it is for low densities, up to about 15 houses per acre; at higher densities, modify the cluster with the additional structure given by [[38. Row Houses]] or [[39. Housing Hill]]. Always provide common land between the houses - [[67. Common Land]] and a shared common workshop [[157. Home Workshop]]. Arrange paths clearly - [[98. Circulation Realms]] - and lay these paths out in such a way that they create busier paths and backwaters, even within the cluster - [[36. Degrees of Publicness]]; keep parking in [[103. Small Parking Lots]],and make the houses in the cluster suit the households which will live there - [[75. The Family]], [[76. House for a Small Family]], [[77. House for a Couple]], [[78. House for One Person]], [[79. Your Own Home]].
