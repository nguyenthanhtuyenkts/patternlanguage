### Problem
>The human body does not wear out with use. On the contrary, it wears down when it is not used.

### Solution
>Scatter places for team and individual sports through every work community and neighborhood: tennis, squash, table tennis, swimming, billiards, basketball, dancing, gymnasium…and make the action visible to passers-by, as an invitation to participate.

### Related Patterns
... all the areas where people live and work - especially the [[41. Work Community]] and the areas looked after by the preventive programs of the [[47. Health Center]] - need to be completed by provisions for sports and exercise. This pattern defines the nature and distribution of this exercise. 

Treat the sports places as a special class of recognizable simple buildings, which are open, easy to get into, with changing rooms and showers - [[95. Building Complex]], [[144. Bathing Room]]; combine them with community swimming pools, where they exist - [[71. Still Water]] ; keep them open to people passing - [[101. Building Thoroughfare]], [[165. Opening to the Street]],and provide places where people can stop and watch - [[241. Seat Spots]], [[243. Sitting Wall]] ...
